Privacy Policy

SleepyPasta Studio · Effective 19 November 2026

SleepyPasta Studio ("the app") is a private tool that uploads videos made by the SleepyPasta and Village Shorts channels to those channels' own YouTube and TikTok accounts. Only the channels' owner signs in to it. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

With your permission the app requests two Google scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload a finished video to your channel, as Private with a scheduled publish time, with its title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel's ID and name, so it can check that it is uploading to the right channel, and to confirm an uploaded video's privacy status.

The app does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, watch history or any other account data, and it never changes or deletes existing videos.

TikTok

With your permission the app requests the TikTok scopes user.info.basic (your account's display name and ID, to show which account is signed in), video.upload and video.publish (to send a short video to your account). TikTok's handling of your data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens Google and TikTok return, and the channel ID, are saved only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than Google's and TikTok's own APIs, are not shared with or sold to anyone, and are not used for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than uploading the owner's videos. The app has no website accounts, sets no cookies and collects no data from people who watch the videos.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until you revoke access or delete them from the computer. Local copies of uploaded videos are deleted from the computer after the video is published.

Revoking access

  • Google: remove the app at your Google Account's third-party access page (security settings).
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, open Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions, and remove the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
