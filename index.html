SleepyPasta Studio

Calm, slow horror stories to fall asleep to, with a short film from the village each day.

SleepyPasta Studio is a small private production tool. It writes, narrates and illustrates long bedtime horror stories for the SleepyPasta YouTube channel, and short videos for the Village Shorts channel and its TikTok account.

What the app does

  • Renders each finished video on the channel owner's own computer.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API to upload that video to the owner's own channel, as Private with a scheduled publish time, and reads the channel's ID and name to make sure it uploads to the right channel.
  • Uses the TikTok Content Posting API to send short videos to the owner's own TikTok account, where the owner reviews and posts them.

The app is used only by the channels' owner to upload to their own channels. It is not offered to the public, and it does not read, change or delete anyone else's videos or data.

See the Privacy Policy for exactly what data the app uses and how to revoke its access, and the Terms of Service.
